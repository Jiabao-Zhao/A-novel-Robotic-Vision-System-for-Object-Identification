# Supplemental diagnostics for the active own-inference run

These CPU-only helpers inspect saved results from `own_inference_v1`. They are separate from its sealed inference source and never modify descriptors, masks, poses, scores, target ledgers, official CSVs or evaluations. Ground-truth poses are not read. They do not establish benchmark accuracy.

`summarize_own_diagnostics.py` writes unique timestamped JSON/Markdown snapshots under `own_inference_v1/diagnostics/`. It includes every official target and requested instance, distinguishes completed targets with saved poses from completed attempts with zero poses, and separates failed, blocked, pending, missing and invalid receipts. It verifies small candidate-report hashes, target-list hashes against pinned metadata, and extra target directories. Candidate errors remain the original exact text. CAD retained counts and finite/alignment evidence come from saved stage receipts, without reopening descriptors or weights. Original geometry/localization time and guarded/adopted attempt time have separate scopes. A valid ICP iteration-cap hit is not classified as an invalid candidate.

`visualize_own_diagnostics.py` writes unique snapshots under `own_inference_v1/auxiliary_diagnostics/`. Its predeclared receipt-order-v2 rule selects, per dataset, the first available terminal case, first case with a saved pose, first multi-instance case, first no-pose case, and first failed/blocked case; duplicate selections are merged. Scores and GT do not select cases. It supports multiple selected poses and no-pose cases, and displays original candidate masks, coarse/refined poses and saved scores. The original projection function and native CAD millimetres/intrinsics are retained. These are unoccluded all-points projections, including hidden/backside points, rather than visibility-correct rendered silhouettes. Manifest-authorized image/calibration aliases and receipt-bound input hashes are checked. Each diagnostic retains the exact helper source copy. Inspect images and save a separate hash-bound review before claiming a visual review.
