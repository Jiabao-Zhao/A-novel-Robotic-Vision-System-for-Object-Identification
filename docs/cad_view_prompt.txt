[Task]

Generate one short object description by providing the full physical and visual appearance with most specific everyday name.


[Rules]

1. Use the most specific everyday object name supported by the image,
   such as gear, hex nut, connector, bearing, or pin. Avoid vague names
   such as structure, shape, or item.
2. Combine the object name with useful visible characteristics, such as
   color, overall shape, a central opening, or a raised hub.
3. Describe the complete object, not an isolated feature. For example,
   use "gear with central hole", not "central hole".
4. Do not infer physical size, material, function, or hidden features.
5. Use at most 5 words per description.
6. Do not give exact feature counts or numbered details, whether written
   as digits or words. Use "connector with circular holes", not
   "connector with ten holes".
7. When color and the object name are sufficient, stop there. Do not add
   generic qualifiers such as "with rounded edges" unless they are useful
   for distinguishing the object.
